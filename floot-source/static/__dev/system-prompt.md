Use built-in Floot AI at low cost for Indonesian copy, with a transparent deterministic fallback if the AI provider is unavailable. Do not claim image/video generation when it is not implemented. Never promise earnings, invent reviews, create fake transactions, store bank/card secrets in the app, or expose customer deliverables without the order's high-entropy token.
